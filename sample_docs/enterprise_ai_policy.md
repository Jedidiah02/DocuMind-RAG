# Enterprise AI Governance & Data Privacy Policy (2026 - 2027)
**Document ID:** POL-AI-2026-V3
**Classification:** Internal Confidential
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Author:** Office of Data Governance & AI Safety Committee

---

## 1. Executive Summary
This policy establishes mandatory operational protocols, compliance standards, and risk mitigations for employees, contractors, and automated agentic systems operating with artificial intelligence. The objective is to maximize productivity while strictly safeguarding proprietary IP, customer personally identifiable information (PII), and regulatory compliance (GDPR, EU AI Act, SOC2 Type II).

## 2. Approved Models and Providers
Employees are permitted to utilize only vetted AI providers through approved enterprise single sign-on (SSO):
1. **Google Gemini Enterprise Suite** (Gemini 2.5 Flash, Gemini 1.5 Pro) - Approved for general reasoning, technical documentation, code generation, and multi-modal analysis.
2. **OpenAI Enterprise** (GPT-4o, o3-mini) - Approved for natural language synthesis and translation.
3. **Internal DocuMind RAG Cluster** - Approved for all internal document retrieval and customer inquiry workflows.

> **Prohibited:** Public consumer AI tiers (free ChatGPT, unauthenticated web bots) where user inputs may be ingested for model training without data confidentiality guarantees.

## 3. Data Classification and Permitted Usage
- **Tier 1 (Public Data):** Marketing brochures, published blogs, press releases. No restrictions on model input.
- **Tier 2 (Internal Business Data):** Team OKRs, internal sprint roadmaps, architecture diagrams. Permitted only in enterprise LLM instances with zero-retention logging agreements.
- **Tier 3 (Confidential & PII):** Customer contact details, credit card numbers, health data, encryption keys. **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** from raw LLM prompt entry. Must undergo automated regex sanitization or synthetic tokenization prior to prompt embedding.

## 4. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Verification
All AI-generated outputs impacting external stakeholders, financial projections, or legal contracts require mandatory human verification:
- Code commits generated by AI must pass peer code review and automated CI unit tests.
- Customer communications must be verified by an authorized account representative before dispatch.
- Algorithmic decisions impacting employee performance, compensation, or termination are forbidden from full automation.

## 5. Security Incident Response
If proprietary code or Tier 3 data is accidentally uploaded to an unauthorized external model:
1. Immediately contact the Security Operations Center at `soc@enterprise.internal` or extension `x4400`.
2. File an incident disclosure within 2 hours of discovery.
3. The AI Safety Committee will trigger immediate token revocation and audit log inspection.
